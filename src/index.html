Circle-

Circles are the only data. Add tool: drag to place a pattern (even spacing, or exact SV spacing with L); right-click while dragging bends the path smoothly from that point, a second right-click on the same spot makes a corner; the wheel adds or removes circles. Right-click (in the field or on the timeline) deletes the selected circles, or the circle under the cursor when nothing is selected. Pressing on a selected circle always moves the selection, whatever tool is active (hold Alt to draw/add from there instead). Selecting: click = one circle; Shift+click = the whole pattern it belongs to (or everything between it and the one circle already selected); Ctrl+click = the entire stream; click empty space to deselect. Dragging from a stream\u2019s last circle continues it with its own spacing: Ctrl+drag = straight, Shift+drag = perfect curve (circular arc), Ctrl+Shift+drag = bezier curve; the new pattern is selected. Draw tool: freehand; with circles selected a new stroke re-draws that section. Bend tool (5 or B): with a run of circles selected, a line is drawn through them; drag the line to reshape the selection, the wheel changes how wide the bend is. Spacing curve, circle count and Alt+wheel (distance spacing) act on the selected circles only. SV points live in the lower strip of the top timeline: Ctrl+click adds one, click opens its menu. Ctrl+C / X / V copy, cut and paste, A cycles the view, G shows the whole selected stream at once.
